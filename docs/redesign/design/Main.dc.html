On every screen
Connected 2 changes not sent Send to dash

1 · Gauges

What the dash shows. Opens by default.
  • Live preview of the selected page — tap a gauge to edit it
  • Gauge: signal it shows, name, warning and danger limits
  • Add, remove, move gauges
  • Pages: add, rename, reorder, remove
  • “Start from a layout” when adding a page (was Templates)
Why first: it is the job people come back for.

2 · Car

What the car sends. Set once, checked now and then.
  • Your car: pick the ECU from the catalogue
  • Signals with live values and a clear “No data”
  • Adjust a reading that looks wrong
  • Raw CAN frames — last row, “for mapping unknown signals”
Why together: picking the car and checking signals is one task.

3 · Dash

The device itself. Rarely touched.
  • Units, brightness
  • Full-screen alert: signal, above / below, value
  • CAN bus speed
  • Backup and restore (file)
  • Update firmware — its own screen, two files, confirm
  • Keyboard shortcuts — laptop only, behind “?”
Why last: risky or rare things live away from the daily loop.

How saving works — two states, never more

On the dash 2 changes not sent
Every edit is kept in this browser the moment you make it, so a Wi-Fi drop loses nothing. It reaches the dash only when you tap Send to dash. The chip opens the list of what is not sent, with Undo.

Safety cues

Warning 105 °C Danger 115 °C Outlined = colours one gauge
Full-screen alert · Oil pressure below 1.0 bar Solid red + frame icon = takes over the dash
Firmware update and restore always ask first, and name what will happen.
